      How CareCompass Works

      Three simple steps to save time, avoid unnecessary travel, and receive confirmed care.

      1

      1. Search for a Medical Service

      Type the specific procedure you need, like an X-ray or pediatric consult, along with your location.

      2

      2. Check Service Verification

      See verified availability indicators, recent timestamps, and avoid facilities with broken equipment or absent specialists.

      3

      3. Choose or Find Alternatives

      If your closest facility lacks the service, our engine instantly recommends confirmed nearby alternatives with accessibility matching.

      Why Service Verification Matters

      A conventional directory only lists hospital names and general hours. CareCompass verifies actual service-level readiness.

      ⚡

      A hospital being open ≠ service is available

      General operating hours don't guarantee that the radiologist is on duty or that the X-ray machine isn't undergoing calibration. We check specific department readiness.

      🕒

      Information Freshness Auditing

      We track the exact timestamp and source of every service report. Records older than 14 days are clearly flagged as unverified so you never rely on stale data.

      🧭

      Transparent Smart Routing

      We never invent ratings or pretend to use black-box AI. We rank alternatives strictly by confirmed availability, physical accessibility, and proximity.
