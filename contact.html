Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why doesn't CareCompass show every clinic as "Available"?

A: Unlike generic search engines that assume a hospital offers every service just because its front doors are unlocked, CareCompass verifies department readiness. If a hospital hasn't confirmed its radiology desk or its X-ray machine is undergoing maintenance, we mark it either UNAVAILABLE or NOT VERIFIED.

Q: What should I do if a service is marked "NOT VERIFIED"?

A: We recommend using the one-click "Call to Confirm" button on the facility card before travelling. A quick 1-minute phone call can save an hour of frustrating travel.

Q: Is CareCompass free to use?

A: Yes. CareCompass is built as a public interest digital healthcare access initiative. It does not require fees, logins, or insurance cards to search.
