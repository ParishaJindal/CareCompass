CareCompass • Digital Healthcare Access and Navigation

Navigating Healthcare with Trust & Precision

CareCompass was founded on a simple realization: A hospital listing that says "Open 24/7" is not the same as having the specific machine, specialist, or service you need right now.

The Critical Problem in Conventional Directories

Why ordinary map apps and static hospital listings fail patients in their hour of need.

❌ The Stale Directory Trap

A patient takes a sick family member across town for an emergency chest X-ray because an online directory showed the hospital was open. Upon arrival, they discover the X-ray tube burned out yesterday and maintenance won't arrive until Monday.

✓ The CareCompass Solution

CareCompass decouples overall hospital status from specific service readiness. If an X-ray is broken, it is prominently flagged CONFIRMED UNAVAILABLE with a direct recommendation to the closest confirmed provider.

Our Core Innovation: Smart Service Verification Contract

How our verification engine guarantees data integrity without making unfounded claims.

CONFIRMED AVAILABLE Verified within the last 14 days by an authoritative source

The specific medical procedure has been confirmed operational by a verified hospital administrator, radiographer on duty, or direct automated telemetry sync.

CONFIRMED UNAVAILABLE Machine down, technician absent, or bed shortage reported

The facility is flagged with an explicit warning banner: "Do NOT travel here for this service." We immediately trigger the Alternative Recommendation Engine.

NOT VERIFIED Record older than 14 days or derived from unconfirmed directories

We NEVER pretend stale data is reliable. Unverified facilities are displayed with caution notices advising patients to call ahead before travelling.

Transparent Recommendation Ranking (No Fake AI)

CareCompass operates on explicit, auditable, deterministic criteria instead of opaque algorithms.

  1. Service Match: Only facilities offering the requested procedure are evaluated.
  2. Unavailability Exclusion: Facilities with confirmed broken equipment or absent specialists are excluded from alternative suggestions.
  3. Availability Priority: Confirmed available facilities are always ranked above unverified ones, regardless of minor distance differences.
  4. Accessibility Alignment: Wheelchair ramps, elevators, and ground floor service access are matched against user preferences.
  5. Transparent Explanation: Every recommendation comes with an explicit reason statement (e.g. "Recommended because X-ray is confirmed available today and facility provides ground-floor wheelchair ramp access").

Accessibility Commitment (WCAG 2.1 AA)

Built to be effortless for elderly patients, screen reader users, and individuals with sensory impairments.

  • Adjustable Font Scaler: Scale typography smoothly to 115% or 130% without horizontal layout overflow.
  • High-Contrast Navy & Yellow Palette: Surpasses WCAG AAA contrast ratios for low-vision readability.
  • Bilingual English & Hindi: Full native language toggle across navigation, search labels, and status badges.
  • Voice Search Integration: Web Speech API input for elderly users who prefer speaking over typing.
  • Visible Keyboard Focus: Distinct 3px cyan focus rings on all interactive elements with logical tab progression.
  • Accessible Touch Targets: Minimum 44x44px interactive areas for mobile and tremor-friendly interaction.

Privacy & Medical Integrity Standards

CareCompass does not require patient accounts or track personal health records. We do not diagnose illnesses or recommend prescription drugs. All location processing is done on the client side with explicit user permission.

⚠️ Medical Emergency Disclaimer: CareCompass is a navigation directory designed to save travel time for non-acute and diagnostic needs. For life-threatening trauma, stroke, cardiac arrest, or severe injury, immediately call national emergency services (108 / 112) or proceed to the nearest trauma emergency room.
